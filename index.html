About

I work on medical image computing and efficient machine learning for resource-constrained settings, building segmentation models that hold up on limited, under-represented data and making models small and fast enough to be useful where compute is scarce.

I'm a master's student in Information Technology (Applied Machine Learning) at Carnegie Mellon University Africa, with a background in Medical Imaging Sciences from the University of Rwanda. Through the SPARK Academy training program I led a team that designed and implemented a solution for the Brain Tumor Segmentation (BraTS) Challenge 2025 under the mentorship of Prof. Mehmet Kurt and Prof. Udunna Anazodo, work that was published at MICCAI 2025.

My current focus is machine learning efficiency for medical use, in medical image computing and biomedical data science. More broadly, I care about the generalizability of AI models and how they fit into real clinical workflows.
